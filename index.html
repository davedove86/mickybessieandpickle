About the stories

Along with their friend Pickle, they inhabit a world where dogs have their own conversations, friendships, disagreements and adventures.

Their stories combine humour, mystery and excitement, exploring the importance of friendship, loyalty and courage.

Their latest adventure unfolds in Nina and the Shadow of Etna, a forthcoming novel for children aged 8–12.

Discover more about Charles Oakley and his forthcoming books at charlesoakley.co.uk.

Meet The Dogs

Micky

Adventurous, mischievous and often inclined to act before thinking. He enjoys teasing his sister Bessie, but his loyalty and courage come to the fore whenever his friends are in danger.

Bessie

More cautious than Micky, Bessie likes to consider the dangers before rushing into an adventure. She loves food and attention, but beneath her occasional anxiety lies intelligence, determination and surprising courage.

Pickle

Curious, sensitive and inventive, Pickle has a talent for noticing things others overlook. His ingenuity often proves invaluable when the dogs find themselves in difficult situations.

Nina

Nina is a young Italian girl, whose courageous, independent and resilient character has been shaped by her life experiences. She shows flashes of good humour and a capacity for positive mischief throughout the adventure.

Her inquisitive nature leads to the first meeting Micky and Bessie but it’s her determination that enables their friendship to develop and endure.

Charles Oakley lives in the North East of England with his family and, of course, dogs.
